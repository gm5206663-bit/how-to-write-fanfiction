# Canon Ledger Template

> The audit trail between canon and your fic. Every chapter's footer carries its ledger rows; this file is the cumulative table. A chapter that consumed canon with no ledger entry is an unaudited chapter.

---

```
# Canon Ledger — <SERIAL NAME>

## Consumed canon

| # | Canon fact / event | Source ch | Used in my ch | Shown or deferred? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | <event> | 62 | 1 | shown |
| 002 | <event> | 63 | 1 | deferred — reason logged in ch footer |

## Butterflies (divergences from canon)

| # | First divergence | Cause (earned by) | Downstream effects tracked |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-01 | my ch 1: <what changed> | OC present at <event> | <ripple list + where logged> |

## Rulings that touched canon handling

| # | Date | Ruling | Effect on spine |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-05 | <date> | "follow canon by default, ask never" | default policy set |
```

**Ledger hygiene rules**

1. One row per canon fact consumed; reference it from the chapter footer.
2. Every butterfly must name its cause — "it would be cool" is not a cause.
3. Downstream effects get their own rows when they land (reference the butterfly #).
4. Deferred canon beats are logged with the reason; "skipped" without a reason is a Multi-Panel Law violation.
5. The gate can diff this ledger against chapter footers — keep them consistent same-turn.
