# foundation/ — the docset that ships before chapter one

**Template · guide v3.0 · law revision v5.4.** Copy into your serial's `foundation/` and rename as you fill. This file doubles as the **docset checklist** and the **rulings log starter**.

**The law behind this file:** a gated serial needs roughly **twenty foundation files before chapter one** (F4) — not as ceremony, but because chapters are written *out of* the docset. Two of them outrank everything else: the **status panel** (single source of truth; if a number isn't in it, it doesn't exist) and the **rulings log** (any unanswered ruling **locks drafting**).

---

## §1 — The docset checklist (~20 files)

**The spine**
- [ ] `FOUNDATION.md` — the base of base: the serial in one page, its lessons, its locks, its world rules
- [ ] `RULINGS_LOG.md` — every author ruling **verbatim**, numbered and dated (starter list in §2)
- [ ] `STATUS_PANEL.md` — the single source of truth ([template](STATUS_PANEL_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `HANDOFF.md` — the cold-start brief: what is true *now*, the authority order, which file wins on conflict

**The system**
- [ ] `SYSTEM_SPEC.md` — the power system complete ([template](SYSTEM_SPEC_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `METERS.md` — every meter, its pace law, its evolution line ([template](METERS_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `SKILLS_CANON.md` — skills described the way the source describes its own ([template](SKILLS_CANON_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `PANELS.md` — panel grammar + the ledger of every line ever printed ([template](PANELS_TEMPLATE.md))

**The world and the cast**
- [ ] `CHARACTERS.md` — the cast, canon and ours ([template](CHARACTERS_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `RELATIONSHIPS.md` — the relation web + the who-knows-what matrix
- [ ] `GLOSSARY.md` — terms (your fandom's, canon-checked)
- [ ] `PLACES.md` — places, canon-checked, receipted
- [ ] `ECONOMY.md` — money, prices, wages — told honestly
- [ ] `CODEX.md` — the file map + decision log

**The receipts and the record**
- [ ] `CANON_GROUND.md` — receipts: nothing enters a chapter unverified (F3)
- [ ] `CANON_STUDY_BEAST_TYPE.md` (or the source study) — the beasts/mechanics of your world, whole (F20)
- [ ] `CANON_LEDGER.md` — canon spine, confidence tags, butterfly registry, date arithmetic ([template](CANON_LEDGER_TEMPLATE.md))
- [ ] `TIMELINE.md` — canon spine receipted + our road dated — the day map for date arithmetic
- [ ] `STORY_ARCS.md` — the arc plan, marked against what shipped
- [ ] `SERIAL_LOG.md` — the work journal
- [ ] `AGENT_EXPERIENCE.md` — the clean-and-clear guide for whoever works here next
- [ ] `OPEN_RULINGS.md` — numbered decisions only the author can make (any open one **locks drafting**)
- [ ] `RAILS.md` — the serial's own laws of record, each with its receipt

**Beside the docset**
- [ ] `LICENSE` — MIT for tooling; derivative-work notice for the prose
- [ ] `NOTICE.md` — the fandom's IP disclaimer — fan-work terms
- [ ] `chapters/` — empty until the rulings are answered, **and that is correct** (foundation stage is a stage)
- [ ] `checks/run_all.sh` + `tools/` — the battery, one command, exit 0 ([guide](../docs/06_automation.md))

## §2 — RULINGS_LOG.md — the starter

Replace with **your** rulings, verbatim and dated. The F-series below is the reference serial's constitution (the Grey Wolf) — kept as a shape and a vocabulary; every serial earns its own list. Numbers are receipts: gaps are reserved — new laws take the next free number.

- **F0** — the OC: an Earth mind reborn, full meta, deliberate start, beside the canon lead — never in front
- **F2** — bloodline at awakening (the F15 model), never granted for ordinary training
- **F3** — research everything; check canon perfectly — receipts, not memory
- **F4** — all files: the docset exists before chapter one
- **F5** — workshop refresh: hygiene, tokens never in the tree
- **F6** — do yourself: when the author calls a rebuild, stop and audit first
- **F7** — full panels: the status panel is the single source of truth; never rebuilt from memory
- **F8** — honest pace: every meter on its own fuel
- **F9** — grade ladder: grades are honest current readings; 100% triggers instant evolution
- **F10** — ring seats beast bloodline: the line deepens on real events, shows in appearance first
- **F11** — full grant: seven parts per ring; white 10 · yellow 100 · purple 1,000 · black 10,000 · red 100,000
- **F12** — walls alone ring-gated; **F13** — honest yield 24/7; nothing purchasable moves a meter
- **F14** — the Evolution Chain: every technique Low → Mid → High → Top, 1–100% within each; at 100% it evolves that second; there is no such thing as 100% MASTERED
- **F15** — interconnection: talent grows as bloodlines grow and techniques evolve (the 2.96× cascade)
- **F16** — thousand-year: rings age in their seats by what pours through them, not by calendar
- **F17** — evolution on breakthrough: the soul and its skills evolve with new names; concealment is lawful and lives in the ledger
- **F18** — life-skills evolve on the same chain — every learned thing is a technique
- **F19** — canon skill format: describe skills the way the source describes its own
- **F20** — beast study: the source's beast whole — never a normal animal
- **F22** — panel prose rule: full panels only on update or gain; 0 lines is legal
- **F23** — the Evolution Chain Fix, pinned (so no edit can quietly lose it)
- **F26** — footwork fuses: Walking + Running + Mountain Stride → ONE line — *which fool masters so many step techniques separately? They fuse!*
- **P12** — the camera law (film/TV fandoms): the camera goes where canon goes
- **Structural** — StoryOS · Control Centre · Soul Library · Dual-Track · Foundation-Stage · Ship Law · Clean-and-clear

*Reserved forever: F1 · F21 · F24 · F25 · F27+ — a renumbered law loses the mistake that paid for it. The full texts, each with its check: [`docs/01_the_laws.md`](../docs/01_the_laws.md).*
