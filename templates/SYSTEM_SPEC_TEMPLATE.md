## §1 — Slots and the full grant (F10 · F11)

- **Slots:** 1 / 1 / 1 — one ring-slot per initial rank; **+1 slot per rank-up**, reaching 10 / 10 / 10 at Titled rank.
- **Ring seats beast bloodline (F10):** every soul ring seats the bloodline of the beast it came from. The line deepens on living releases, hunts, and years — and on the ring's own age (see §4). It feeds blood, body, soul, vitality, recovery, the predator's frame, and the senses; it changes the appearance before it changes any number.
- **Full grant (F11):** a ring grants everything at once — **skill · rank · gift · soul's upgrade · body flood · blood · years.** Ring-color years: **white 10 · yellow 100 · purple 1,000 · black 10,000 · red 100,000.** First-ring absorption limit ~420 years — but once seated, a ring **ages beyond it.**
- **Walls (F12):** rank bottlenecks are walls; a wall yields only to a ring, earned through the trials the canon defines, **alone.** Nothing else moves it — not money, not time, not wishing.
- **Honest yield (F13):** training yields around the clock — passive gains are real, quiet, and logged; nothing is purchasable and there is no ceiling.

## §2 — The evolution chain (F14 · F23)

**There is no such thing as terminal.** Every technique has a grade — **Low · Mid · High · Top** — and advances 1–100% within its grade. **At 100%, that same second, it evolves** into the next technique of its lineage. Every grade carries a different name.

- Basic Walking (Low) 1–100% → **Silent Wind Step** (Mid) 1–100% → **Ghost Frost Storm Step** (High) 1–100% → **Storm Frost Ghost Veil** (Top)
- Running (Low) → Wind Stride (Mid) → Storm Step (High)
- Examples by family: Body Control → Flowing Body → Storm Body · Five Senses → Keen Senses → Spirit Eye · Reading → Fluent Reading → Deep Reading · Cooking → Camp Cooking → Feast Cooking
- **Fusion:** 2–3 suitable Lows (each 80%+ , or one 100% + two 60%+) fuse → one Mid at 1%; 2–3 Mids → one High. Logic decides what fuses: movement + movement + body = movement; mental + mental + mental = mental; perception + perception + patience = perception. **Footwork fuses to ONE line** — Walking + Running + Mountain Stride → Silent Wind Step (Mid) — never three separate Highs (F26).
- **Replaces the old model:** "mastery, no stages → MASTERED" — that was nonsense and is superseded. The author's own words: *"There is no such thing: 100% MASTERED"*; *"basic walking and running low it go 1 to 100% the just as it reach 100% that second it evolve into next like imagine silent wind step, mid level."*

## §3 — Interconnection (F15)

Effective talent is not fixed — it **grows as the bloodlines grow and the techniques evolve**: innate 1 + bloodlines + every basic moving through its chain + fusions. One meter rising visibly moves others (the recorded cascade: a Mid appearance at 2.96× effect). *Example: effective talent ~3.5×, Spirit Sea 850.* The whole is faster than its parts can account for — and the exact-figures section of the status panel is where the stacking is stated honestly.

## §4 — Thousand-year aging (F16)

Rings age **in their seats — not by the calendar, but by what pours through them.** *Example: the reference serial's Ghost ring went 120 → 1,350 years (purple) by its Ch5 gate; the second seat reached 1,850.* Thousand-year rank puts everything at Mid or above and fuses the hunt into a named method (Grey Ridge Hunt in the example). The ring's age is a ledger entry; the gate fails a stale age.

## §5 — Evolution on breakthrough (F17) and concealment

When bloodline change and a ring-grade breakthrough land (yellow → purple at 1,000 years; purple → black at 10,000), the **martial soul evolves** (*example: Grey Wolf → Ice Wolf → Storm Frost Ghost Wolf*, one lineage, different names per grade), and **skills upgrade** — keeping identity, gaining power, gaining a new effect, under the evolved name. **Concealment law:** the *Ring Veil* shows a true purple ring as yellow to the world; the true color lives in the ledger. Canon shows true ring colors to the reader; the veil belongs to the character, **never to the narrator's lie.**

## §6 — Life-skills (F18)

Everything a person learns is a technique — soul-power control, body control, the five senses, observation, running, walking, stillness, hunter's sense, mountain stride, spear, tally, plain speech, reading, understanding, spearmanship, cooking, combat style. Each has its grade and its 1–100%; each evolves with a new name; suitable ones fuse. **The meters and their pace laws live in [`METERS.md`](METERS_TEMPLATE.md)** — this file states the law, that file tracks the lives.
