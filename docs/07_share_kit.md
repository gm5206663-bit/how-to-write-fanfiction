## The essay version (AO3/AO3-adjacent, blogs)

Title: **"Canon Is a River: What I Learned Machine-Gating 796,000 Words of Fanfiction"**

Beats:
1. The origin: a 90,000-word serial rejected for five reasons that were all diagnosable in hindsight.
2. The reversal: stop treating canon as vibes; treat it as a state machine — panel, ledger, gate.
3. The laws that were paid for (each with its receipt): canon-first; OC added, never centered; traits are dated states; nothing pre-decided; one truth, zero copies.
4. The humbling part: the gates catch the author most of all — stale numbers, private vocabularies, prose tics, a checker that could never fire.
5. What it costs: slower chapters. What it buys: a story that can grow forever without rotting.
6. The invitation: the method is MIT-licensed; the gate example self-tests; steal it.
